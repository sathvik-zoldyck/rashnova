# Getting help with Rashnova

**Start here:**

- **[README](README.md):** what Rashnova does, how to install it and how to check the download.
- **[Known limits](KNOWN_LIMITS.md):** what Rashnova does not do, or does not do yet. Many
  questions are answered there.
- **[FAQ on the website](https://www.alcyonesecure.com/faq).**

**Still stuck?** Write to **support@alcyonesecure.com**. Tell us your Rashnova version (Settings),
your Windows version, what you did and what you saw. A screenshot of an error message helps.

**Found a bug?** [Open an issue](https://github.com/sathvik-zoldyck/rashnova/issues/new/choose).

> [!CAUTION]
> Rashnova's record is about your computer and the people who use it. **Never post your record,
> a report, file names, folder names, account names or anything personal** in a public issue. If
> we need more detail, we will ask you privately.

**Security problem?** Do not open an issue. Follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md).

**Forgot your PIN?** It cannot be recovered, by us or anyone else: a way to recover it would be a
way around it. Switching always-on recording off never needs the PIN.
